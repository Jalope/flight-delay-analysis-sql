# Day 3 - Findings

## Weekly flight distribution

The analysis shows that Monday concentrates the highest number of flights globally. The pattern is consistent across the largest states:

* California
* Texas
* Florida

This indicates that the weekly flight distribution is not driven by a specific region but is a general pattern across the dataset.

## Month flight distribution

Although the dataset is incomplete (containing data only for January and February) we cannot proceed with a full monthly flight distribution. However, we observed that January's figures exceed February's in most cases, likely because February is three days shorter.

Additionally, percentage analysis shows that January accounts for approximately 52% of total flights, while February represents around 48%. This confirms that the observed difference is moderate and largely explained by the shorter duration of February rather than a significant structural change in flight activity.
